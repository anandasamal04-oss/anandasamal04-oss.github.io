Privacy Policy for Cow Dairy Hisaab

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Cow Dairy Hisaab ("the App") is a dairy management application designed to help users maintain and manage their daily dairy and cattle-related records. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information.

1. Information We Collect

The App may allow you to enter information related to your dairy activities, such as:

  • Cattle and animal records
  • Milk production records
  • Milk sales and income records
  • Feed and expense records
  • Other dairy management information entered by you

The information you enter is used to provide the features and functionality of the App.

2. Data Storage

Your dairy records may be stored locally on your device. If you uninstall the App, reset your device, or lose your device, locally stored data may be lost. Users are responsible for maintaining backups of their important information.

3. Backup

If the App provides a backup feature, users may choose to back up their application data to a cloud storage service such as Google Drive. The backup is initiated by the user and is intended to help prevent loss of important dairy records.

The App does not sell your dairy records or personal information to third parties.

4. Personal Information

The App does not require users to provide personal information unless such information is necessary for a feature that the user chooses to use.

5. Google Drive and Third-Party Services

If you choose to use Google Drive backup, the App may interact with Google Drive to store or retrieve your backup data. Your use of Google services is also subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the App. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or dairy records. Information may only be handled by third-party services when necessary to provide a feature that you choose to use, such as cloud backup.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for general use and is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization.

9. Your Data

You can manage the information stored in the App through the features provided by the App. You may delete locally stored records using the App's available functions.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the Cow Dairy Hisaab App, please contact the developer through the contact information provided with the application.
